# Resume Routing Template

## Volume Mode

- Pick one stable default resume for each role family.
- Avoid tailoring during broad-volume runs unless the role is unusually high fit.
- Confirm the selected file path before every upload.

## Suggested Routing Table

| Role family | Default resume | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Data Analyst | `[default analytics resume]` | Data Analyst, Insights Analyst, Analytics Analyst |
| Business Analyst | `[default BA resume]` | Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, Operations Analyst |
| Product / Operations | `[default product or ops resume]` | Product Analyst, Product Operations, GTM Analyst |

## Precision Mode

- Promote only specific high-fit jobs into tailored editing.
- Keep the editable source separate from upload-ready PDFs.
