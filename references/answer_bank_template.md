# Answer Bank Template

Use truthful, reusable patterns only. Replace placeholders with candidate-specific facts in a private companion workflow.

## Work Authorization

- Current authorization: `I am currently authorized to work in the United States on [status].`
- Sponsorship: `I require sponsorship.` or `[candidate-specific wording]`

## Location

- Current location: `Based in [city, state].`
- Relocation: `Open to relocation.`
- Work mode: `Open to remote, hybrid, and onsite opportunities.`

## Compensation

- Range: `I’m targeting a base salary in the range of [range], depending on scope, location, and total compensation package.`

## Start Date

- Earliest start: `[month year or exact date]`

## Why This Company

- Draft from mission, product, business model, and role overlap.

## Why This Role

- Draft from analytics, stakeholder partnership, experimentation, reporting, or business impact overlap.

## Domain-Specific Questions

- CRM tools: describe real usage only.
- Industry experience gaps: acknowledge adjacent transferable work and willingness to learn quickly.
- Fast-paced environment: emphasize prioritization, clear communication, and adaptability.
