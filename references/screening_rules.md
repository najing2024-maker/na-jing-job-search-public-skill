# Screening Rules

## Prioritize

- Data Analyst and Business Analyst roles
- Entry to early or mid-level roles
- Los Angeles, San Francisco Bay Area, or U.S. remote positions
- Fresh postings first, ideally within 24 to 48 hours
- Short, low-friction ATS flows in volume mode

## Consider

- Product or operations roles with strong analytics overlap
- Hybrid or onsite roles when relocation is acceptable
- Slightly older roles when fit is unusually strong
- Moderate stretch roles that are not clearly overleveled

## Skip

- Explicit no-sponsorship language
- Unrestricted U.S. work authorization requirements that conflict with the candidate
- Citizenship or clearance requirements
- Senior, staff, principal, director, or manager roles
- Weak-fit roles with long ATS friction
- Duplicate, closed, or broken application paths

## Hand Off

- CAPTCHA or anti-bot checks
- Login or two-factor authentication
- Required uploads the browser cannot handle safely
- High-impact legal or compensation questions not already stored
- Writing samples, portfolios, or references not yet prepared
